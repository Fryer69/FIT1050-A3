About Us

The Society of Art and Drawing Appreciation became affiliated with Nosham Clubs & Societies on the 2nd of November, 2006. It initially aimed to bring together the many arts fans at Nosham (yes, you can hide, but we know you're there!), with its numerous weekly social activities and special events, and to help members greet, meet, and form friendships that will last a lifetime.
